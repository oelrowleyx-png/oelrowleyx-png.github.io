Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Clip Saver ("the app") is a private, internal tool operated by its owner for a small team. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Google user data

The app uses the Google Drive API with the drive.file permission only. This means it can only see and change files and folders that the app itself created in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot read any other files in the account.

With that permission the app creates a folder, uploads video files into dated subfolders, and creates and updates one spreadsheet that logs each saved video and its caption.

Google user data is used only to provide these features. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Other data

To control access, the app stores the Telegram user ID and display name of team members the owner has approved. Video links, captions and files are stored on the owner's private server and Google Drive.

Retention and deletion

Video files are deleted automatically after 7 days. The owner can remove the app's access at any time from their Google Account under Security → Third-party connections, and can delete the app's folder and spreadsheet from Google Drive.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
